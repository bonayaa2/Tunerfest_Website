Tuner Fest 6.0

Event rules

The terms and conditions for Tuner Fest 6.0 on November 29, 2026 at Galleria Mall, Karen. They apply to show car drivers, exhibitors and everyone attending.

Last updated October 29, 2026

1. No burnouts

Burnouts are not allowed anywhere at the event, including the parking areas, the roads around the venue and on the way in or out.

2. Revving only on the top silo parking

Revving is only allowed on the top level of the silo parking. Don't rev anywhere else at the venue.

3. Listen to the marshals

Marshals are there to keep everyone safe. If a marshal tells you to stop revving, move your car or change what you're doing, do it straight away.

4. No outside alcohol

You can't bring your own alcohol into the event. Alcohol is only available to buy at the venue, from the event's official alcohol vendor.

5. Damage to other people's cars

If you damage someone else's car, you are responsible for paying for the repairs.

6. Damage to your own car

Shift Nation and Al-Husnain Motors are not liable for any damage to your car at the event, whether you cause it yourself, another attendee causes it, or it happens any other way. Show cars must be insured.

7. Photos and video

Tuner Fest is a public event. Your car, stand and products may be photographed, filmed and posted online by Shift Nation and by other people attending. How we handle your personal data is explained in our Privacy policy.

8. Breaking the rules

If you break these rules, the marshals or the Shift Nation team may ask you to leave the event.

Questions

Email info@shiftnation.org before the event if anything here is unclear.

Your data

Privacy

What we collect when you register a car or a booth, why, who can see it, and when we delete it.

Last updated October 29, 2026

Who we are

Tuner Fest is organised by Shift Nation, under Al-Husnain Motors. Together we decide how your data is used, so we are responsible for it under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019. For anything about your data, email info@shiftnation.org.

What we collect

  • Show car registration: your name, email, phone number, Instagram handle (optional), the name of your car, and a photo of it.
  • Booth request: your name, email, phone number, business name, what you'll show or sell, booth size, whether you need power, and any notes.
  • Driver passes: the pass code, the driver's name and car, and the time the pass is scanned at the gate.

Tickets are sold by our ticketing partner Little Africa, not on this website, so their own privacy terms apply to ticket purchases.

Why we use it

  • To review your application and tell you by email whether it's approved.
  • To send your driver pass and check you in at the show car entrance.
  • To contact you about the event, such as roll-in times and setup details.

We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

What's public

If your car is approved, its photo and name appear on our Show cars page. Your name, email, phone number and Instagram handle are never shown. Photos of pending or declined cars stay private. Everything else is seen only by the Shift Nation team.

Who helps us run the site

These services store or send data for us, only to run the website and the event. Some of them keep data on servers outside Kenya.

  • Supabase: our database and photo storage.
  • Vercel: hosts the website.
  • Resend and Zoho Mail: send and receive our emails.
  • Google Fonts: provides the website's fonts, so Google sees your device's IP address when the page loads.

How long we keep it

We keep your data only until the event is over. After Tuner Fest 6.0 on November 29, 2026, we delete applications, car photos and driver passes.

Cookies

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't run analytics. The only things this site stores in a browser are the sign-in for Shift Nation team members, so they stay logged in to the admin pages, and the gate link on marshals' phones.

Event photos

Tuner Fest is a public event. When you register, you agree that your car or stand may be photographed and filmed, and posted online by Shift Nation and by other people attending.

Our gallery shows photos from past Shift Nation events, which may include people and cars. If you're in a photo and want it removed, email info@shiftnation.org and we'll take it down.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it at any time, including withdrawing your application. Email info@shiftnation.org. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) in Kenya.
